# Impact Case

## Problem

Digital betting products compete for attention: many banners, many games,
many markets — all at once. Users who arrive with a clear goal ("Real Madrid",
"diamonds", "Book of …") still wade through irrelevant promotion, while users
who struggle get no help and users at risk get nudged at exactly the wrong
moment.

## Solution

PSK Intelligence turns existing PSK surfaces into **context-aware content
surfaces**. It reads privacy-safe, in-session signals (searches, viewed
events, friction patterns), decides with an explicit WHY, and adapts only
through behaviour — reorder, prioritise, defer, simplify, or stay silent.

- **Before:** many things competing for attention.
- **After:** the right existing PSK content gets priority at the right moment.

## Demonstrable outcomes (in the prototype)

1. **Find faster** — typo-tolerant entity search (`Madird` → Real Madrid)
   moves the relevant existing hero banner into the primary position and
   hands context over to the match page.
2. **Understand easier** — MEDIUM friction reduces density (SIMPLIFY);
   productive exploration is deliberately *not* treated as friction.
3. **Act clearly** — once a bet is selected, the final surface shows exactly:
   Selection, Stake, Total odds, Potential return, Payment method, CONFIRM.
   No tokens, no pre-charging, no urgency.
4. **Leave easily** — after completion the engine returns to `NONE` and shows
   no immediate follow-up gambling promotion.

## Responsible-by-design impact

- An **independent Responsible Gate** (PASS / ADAPT / SILENCE) sits outside
  the decision engine and can suppress any adaptation, including in
  high-friction or at-risk sessions.
- Reduced-motion, hidden-tab and interaction-priority rules are honoured
  everywhere motion exists.

## Measured honesty

All performance figures shown in the judge panel are measured in the browser
at runtime; where data is unavailable the panel says "Unavailable in
prototype" instead of inventing numbers. Dataset evidence is labelled
aggregate/derived with provenance and explicit limitations.
